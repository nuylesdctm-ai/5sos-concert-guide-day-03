MOA Arena Concert Day & Survival Guide

🚨 Emergency Hotlines

Tap directly to call:

📞 National Emergency: 911 🚑 Red Cross: 143 / (02) 8527-0000 👮 Pasay City Police: (02) 8831-1544 🏢 MOA Arena Security: (02) 8470-2222

📍 Entrances & Gates

  • VIP Standing / Patron: Ground Floor Entrances (Coral Way or Marina Way side).
  • Lower Box, Upper Box, Gen Ad: 2nd Floor Elevated Bridge / Walkway connecting from SM Mall of Asia.
  • Baggage Counters: Available near Coral Way and Marina Way ground gates (Expect long lines after the show).

🚻 Restrooms & Medical Aid

  • CRs: Available on every floor near stairwells/exits. Tip: Mas mabilis mag-CR sa loob ng SM MOA bago pumila sa Arena gates.
  • First Aid / Clinic: Ground Floor Backstage corridor & Level 2 lobby near main guest services.

⚠️ Bag Policy & Reminders

  • Maximum bag size: 12 x 12 inches. Backpacks are usually subject to check/holding.
  • Strictly no re-entry.
  • No outside food and beverages; no pro cameras (DSLR/mirrorless).
